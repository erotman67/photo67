About

I'm Elad, a photographer from Israel. I shoot close up and mostly outdoors — water on a leaf, a coin in the rain, a wasp that stayed still for one frame. What I'm really after is light: what it does to glass, to a drop of water, to the sky behind a mast at the end of the day. I take the photographs and edit them myself.
